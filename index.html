Support & help

Your workout companion, your choices

FitGenius is an iPhone exercise reference app developed by Michael Coulter. You can browse the bundled exercise list, filter by muscle group, and save a routine on your device. Optional video tutorials use YouTube API Services. This policy explains both the app's local storage and the information third parties receive when you use those services.

Information kept on your device

FitGenius stores your routine's exercise names, muscle-filter and drawer preferences, and your video-privacy choice in the app's local web storage. A session flag helps reset filters on a fresh launch. These selections are not sent to Michael Coulter or uploaded to a FitGenius server. Device backups managed by Apple may include app data, depending on your settings.

Remove exercises individually or select Clear routine to delete your saved routine. Reset Selection resets the muscle filters. Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading the app can retain it. Backup copies are controlled through your Apple settings.

FitGenius has no account system, developer-operated backend, analytics SDK, or advertising SDK. It does not access Apple Health, location services, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or motion sensors. It does not record workouts, body measurements, or medical information.

Optional YouTube videos

No YouTube player or Player API is loaded until you select Agree and enable videos. You can use the catalog and routine without enabling videos. After agreement, opening an exercise video connects to Google/YouTube, even before you press Play.

Google receives network and device information, such as your IP address, browser/operating-system details and app origin, along with requested video IDs, playback interactions and technical diagnostics. It may use cookies or similar identifiers and infer approximate location from IP addresses. Google processes this information to deliver and measure its service, protect against abuse, and serve advertising. FitGenius does not receive a copy of Google's viewer data or request access to your Google account.

Players use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. Google says this mode serves non-personalized ads and does not use embedded views to personalize subsequent YouTube browsing or advertising outside the app. It still involves data collection. FitGenius does not suppress YouTube's ads or player controls.

By enabling videos, you agree to this policy and the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's processing and sharing are governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Google sets its own retention periods; FitGenius cannot delete information held by Google. Use Google's privacy controls to manage available account data and deletion options.

To stop opening embedded videos, choose Menu → About → Disable YouTube videos. This removes the saved agreement and closes players; it does not erase information already received by Google or all website storage. Closing a video, leaving its screen, or backgrounding the app removes the active player.

External links and support

Opening a link, including Watch on YouTube, the privacy policy or a Google privacy control, sends a request to that destination. Its policies apply, and existing browser or app sign-ins may affect its processing. The FitGenius policy and support site are hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security. The site contains no added analytics, advertising, or contact form.

If you email support, you choose to share your email address and message with Michael Coulter and the email providers that deliver it. Support correspondence is used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive health information. To ask about or request deletion of support correspondence, contact the address below; any required retention will be explained when responding.

Audience and policy changes

FitGenius is a general fitness reference, not a service directed to children under 13. It does not ask users to provide an age or create an account. Third-party services have their own age requirements. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us.

Updates to this policy will appear at this URL with a revised date. If a change materially expands the app's video-data practices, the app will request a new agreement before enabling that use.
