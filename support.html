Privacy Policy

Get in touch

For help, feedback, or an unavailable video, email Michael Coulter at coulter.software45@gmail.com. Include the exercise name, app version and iPhone model if relevant. Please do not send sensitive health information.

Find an exercise

Open the muscle drawer, choose a muscle group, and close the drawer to browse. Selecting more than one muscle shows exercises matching every selected group. Use Reset Selection to return to the full catalog.

Watch a tutorial

Tap an exercise title. The first time, review the privacy notice and choose Agree and enable videos. Then use YouTube's player controls to start the tutorial, turn on captions when available, or enter full screen. An internet connection is required. Videos belong to their respective creators; FitGenius and the creators are independent.

If a video will not play, check your connection, close and reopen it, or choose Watch on YouTube. Videos can become unavailable or restricted by their owners or YouTube. You can disable embedded videos in Menu → About.

Keep a routine handy

Tap + beside an exercise to add it. Open Menu → My Routine to view your saved list and its tutorials. Tap − to remove an exercise or Clear routine to start again. Your routine is stored locally; deleting the app removes its local data.

Exercise within your ability

FitGenius is an exercise reference, not medical advice or a personalized training prescription. Choose movements appropriate to your ability and seek qualified guidance when you need help with technique.
